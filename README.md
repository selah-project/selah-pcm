# Di Hebrew Bible — Naija Pidgin Version

Verse-by-verse rendering of di Hebrew Bible (Tanakh) — faithful to
di letters of di original text.

## Hebrew first

Dis version dey here to carry you go di Hebrew — no be to take im
place. Wetin person dey read na di Hebrew — im consonants, im vowel
signs, im accent marks. Di Pidgin wey dey under am na window, no be
replacement. Anywhere wey dem look like sey dem no gree, na di
Hebrew correct.

## Di Name

Anywhere wey di four-letter Name show, we dey write **Yahweh** — we
no dey swap am for title, we no dey turn am to common word. We no
dey write **GOD** or **LORD** (dat one na to wipe di Name wit
title — title no be name), we no dey write **Jehovah** (mixed
form), and we no dey put **Oga** or **Baba God** for di Name seat.
Name remain name, everywhere, as e be — so dat you go fit see where
di Name dey, every time.

## Aleph-tav — ⟨את⟩

Inside di Pidgin sentence you go see **⟨את⟩** wey dem keep am. For
Hebrew, אֵת na small word wey dey point di direct object; Pidgin no
get word wey match am, and plenty translations dey remove am
quiet-quiet. We dey leave am make e show inside bracket — because
na part of di structure of di language. Instead of to wipe am, we
choose to show sey e dey there.

## Words inside ⟨bracket⟩

Bracket ⟨like dis⟩ dey show words **wey no dey inside di Hebrew** —
na additions wey Pidgin grammar need make di sentence stand. Instead
of to mix dem in wit no mark, we dey put dem inside bracket, so dat
di Hebrew own words and di words wey Pidgin dey demand go fit
separate, always.

## Names and titles

Di Hebrew words for di divine — **Elohim**, **El**, **Shaddai**,
**Adonai**, **Yah** — dem dey written wit dia own sound as dem be;
we no squeeze all of dem enter one word. Each one carry im own
weight. Names of people and place too dey come from di Hebrew form:
Mitsrayim (Egypt), Paroh (Pharaoh), Moshe (Moses), Shaul.

## Marked words

- **chesed** (חֶסֶד) — covenant faithful-love. E no be only
  "kindness", e no be only "love"; no single Pidgin word fit carry
  am.
- **Sheol** (שְׁאוֹל) — di place of di dead as di Hebrew talk am,
  no be "hell".
- **nefesh**, **ruach**, **kavod** — dem dey rendered by context
  (soul or life, spirit or wind, glory or weight) — no be wit one
  stiff word.

## Every verse for im own light

Every verse dey rendered as e talk for im own place — read inside
di one-Elohim faith of di Hebrew, wit no later doctrine put inside,
wit no reading di end of di story enter im beginning. To let di
words mean wetin dem mean for dia own place — na dat be di aim.

## Structure of di files

One file for each verse: di verse itself, and word-by-word gloss
arranged side-by-side wit di Hebrew — so dat every Hebrew word go
fit read face-to-face wit im Pidgin partner.
